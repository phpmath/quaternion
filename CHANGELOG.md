## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-23

### Added

- First release.
- Immutable quaternions for 3D rotation: axis-angle and Euler angle constructors, `rotate()`, `toRotationMatrix()`, `slerp()` and `isSameRotation()`.
- A documentation site at https://phpmath.github.io/quaternion/.
